## What this app does

Visiontrack-ai finds objects in your camera feed. The app uses artificial intelligence to identify items in real-time. It detects common things like people, chairs, laptops, and bottles. You see the results inside the app window as labels on your screen. This tool works locally on your computer. Your camera feed stays on your device.

## 💻 System requirements

The app works on Windows 10 and Windows 11. 

- Memory: 8GB RAM minimum.
- Processor: Intel Core i5 or equivalent.
- Camera: A built-in or USB webcam.
- Storage: 500MB of free disk space.
- Graphics: Standard integrated or dedicated graphics card.

## 🚀 Running the application

Open the application using the shortcut on your desktop. When the app starts, it asks for permission to access your webcam. Click "Allow" so the AI can see the camera feed. 

Once the camera turns on, the app identifies objects in the frame. A box appears around each item. The app labels each object and displays the confidence level.

## 🛠️ How to use the settings

The menu at the top of the interface provides several options:

- Camera Selection: Choose which camera to use if you have more than one.
- Detection Sensitivity: Move the slider to change how strictly the AI identifies objects. Higher settings find more items but might create more errors.
- Save Logs: The app saves detection history to a database file. You can toggle this feature off to save disk space.

## 📁 Data and storage

The app uses a small database to record detection events. This file lives in your Documents folder. The app stores the names of the objects it found and the time of the event. It does not store actual photos or video recordings of your home or office. You can delete this folder at any time to clear your history.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting common problems

Most issues relate to camera permissions or hardware performance.

- The app shows a black screen: Ensure no other program uses your webcam. Close software like Skype, Zoom, or Teams and restart the app.
- The app runs slowly: Close background applications that use high CPU memory. These include web browsers with many tabs or video editing software.
- The app does not detect items: Make sure your webcam lens is clean and the lighting in the room is bright. The AI requires clear images to function.
- The installer fails: Check your internet connection. If the issue keeps happening, restart your computer and try downloading the file again.

## 📋 Privacy information

This software performs all processing on your local machine. It does not send images to a server or cloud service. The detection process happens inside the app memory. When you close the app, the camera turns off completely. You maintain total control over your video data.

## 📖 Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection?
No. You only need an internet connection to download the app. Once installed, the app runs entirely offline.

Does it work with USB webcams?
Yes. The app detects most standard USB cameras. If your camera is not detected, check your device settings in Windows.

Can I move the windows around?
Yes. You can resize the app window to fit your needs. The detection feed scales to match the window size.

How do I remove the app?
Open your Windows Settings, go to Apps, find the app in the list, and select Uninstall. This removes the program files and your settings. You may manually delete the database folder in your Documents if you wish to wipe the history logs.
